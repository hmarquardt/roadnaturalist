About this workspace

Road Naturalist is an early research workspace. The sample corridor is synthetic and demonstrates the interface only. It is not a recommendation, road access finding, wildlife record, or mapped habitat analysis.

Future GIS results will carry source, version, coverage, and computation details. An Investigator may interpret and check them, but cannot create authoritative facts.
