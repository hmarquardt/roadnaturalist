About this workspace

Road Naturalist is an early research workspace. The pilot corridors use real road-centerline geometry from the U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025 ROADS dataset, real U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service National Wetlands Inventory polygons, and real U.S. Geological Survey hydrography lines, so their EPA Level III/IV ecological context and their wetland and surface-water surroundings are calculated from real Oregon GIS data. Nothing here is a recommendation, a road-access finding, a wildlife record, or a habitat-quality score.

Geometry is not access. A named road in a government centerline dataset proves that the road is mapped, not that the public may legally or practically drive it. A mapped road/water intersection is a geometric crossing, not a bridge, a ford, or current water. Access stays unverified until the Investigator checks it.

Future GIS results will carry source, version, coverage, and computation details. An Investigator may interpret and check them, but cannot create authoritative facts.
